1: How to read?
To read this documentation look for the number and tag. The tag is the topic that is discussed under the number. Look for the number below the given list and below it will contain all relevant information regarding the topic. For users look for the "*" symbol besides the number that will indicate a suggested topic.


2: Concept
Space invaders remastered as "College Invaders" with students and faculty as defenders, administration and deans as regular aliens, and trustees as the UFO. The player should be able to shoot a book that when it hits a trustee makes them explode. The blood should not reset after every set of aliens. 


3:* Controls
Shoot- spacebar, the player icon will shoot a projectile from the middle of the icon directly forwards until it hits a alien, UFO, or the edge of the screen
Move left- left arrow key, the player icon will shift left
Move right- right arrow key, the player icon will shift right
